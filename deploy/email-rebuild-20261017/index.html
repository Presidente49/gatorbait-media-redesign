Gators Stun No. 7 Texas 27-24 in Austin Behind Jones Jr.
By Brenden Martin
Florida trailed 17-3, then scored 24 straight points and left DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium with a 27-24 win over No. 7 Texas. The Gators are 5-2 overall and 3-2 in the SEC.
Tramell Jones Jr. threw for two touchdowns and ran for 41 yards, Jadan Baugh ran for 91 yards and a score, and Florida did not turn the ball over.
