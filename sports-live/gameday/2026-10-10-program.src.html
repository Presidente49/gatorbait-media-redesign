Game day guide

Oct. 10, 2026

Kickoff and TV

12:45 p.m. ET at Ben Hill Griffin Stadium, Gainesville.

TV: SEC Network. UF graduate Tori Petry is the sideline reporter.

National radio: ESPN. Former Gators lineman Max Starks calls his first game from The Swamp as analyst.

Source: UAA, The Opening Kickoff, Oct. 9

Getting in

Gates open at 10:45 a.m. Concessions are 50% off from 10:45 to 11:45 a.m.

Express Entry (enter with your face, not your phone) at Gates 1, 4, 7, 8, 12 and 16.

Clear bag policy: clear bag up to 12 by 6 by 12 inches, a one-gallon clear resealable bag or a clutch up to 4.5 by 6.5 inches. No umbrellas.

Source: UAA game day policies

Homecoming

Sold out: the 22nd straight sellout at Ben Hill Griffin Stadium.

Mr. Two Bits: basketball greats Vernon Maxwell and Andrew Moten lead the cheer before the game.

500 games: Saturday is Florida's 500th game at Steve Spurrier-Florida Field since the stadium opened in 1930.

Sources: UAA

Hall of Fame, Class of 2026

Inducted Friday night: Dedric Dukes (track and field), Vernon Hargreaves (football), Vernon Maxwell (basketball), Mikey Meagher (lacrosse), Andrew Moten (basketball), Logan Shore (baseball), Bridget Sloan (gymnastics) and Kelsey Stewart (softball).

Source: UAA

Three storylines

  • A response game. Florida comes off a 45-17 loss at Missouri, Jon Sumrall's first as Gators coach. South Carolina has lost three straight and eight straight SEC games dating to last season.
  • Get Baugh going. Jadan Baugh had five straight 100-yard games before he was held to 13 yards on 12 carries at Missouri. He has 11 rushing touchdowns in five games.
  • Stop the run. South Carolina leads the SEC with 256.0 rushing yards a game. Matt Fuller (433 yards) and quarterback LaNorris Sellers (391) have combined for 14 of the Gamecocks' 21 touchdowns.

Sources: UAA, The Opening Kickoff; ESPN

Up next for the Gators

Florida plays at No. 1 Texas on Oct. 17 and meets No. 2 Georgia in Jacksonville on Oct. 31.

Players to watch

RB Jadan Baugh, Florida (No. 13). 11 rushing touchdowns in five games. Tim Tebow's school record is 23 in a season.

WR Dallas Wilson, Florida (No. 6). Team leader with 18 catches for 302 yards.

QB LaNorris Sellers, South Carolina (No. 16). 1,059 passing yards and 391 rushing yards.

RB Matt Fuller, South Carolina (No. 28). 433 rushing yards, fourth in the SEC.

WR Nyck Harbor, South Carolina (No. 8). 6-5, 239. Loren Meadows calls him and Sellers the most athletic duo Florida has seen this season.

Sources: UAA, The Opening Kickoff; GatorBait Media

Availability report

Florida. Out: RB Kelvin Jimenez (knee). Questionable: WR Vernell Brown III (knee), a game-time decision. Probable: RB London Montgomery, CB Dijon Johnson (ankle).

South Carolina. Out: DB Jalewis Solomon, DL Kobby Sakyi-Prah, OL Josiah Thompson, WR Jayden Sellers. Doubtful: OL Carter Miller, DB Quay'sheed Scott, DL Julian Walker, DL Gabriel Brownlow-Dindy. Questionable: WR Mazeo Bennett Jr.

Source: UAA, updated Friday morning. The SEC's final report posts about 90 minutes before kickoff; check gatorbaitmedia.com.

The series

31-10-3
Florida's lead in the series. Saturday is the 45th meeting and the first since 2023. The Gators are 17-2 against South Carolina in Gainesville.

Last meeting: Florida 41, South Carolina 39, on Oct. 14, 2023, in Columbia. The Gators erased a 10-point fourth-quarter deficit.
