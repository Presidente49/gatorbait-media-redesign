# Community Director

Own the GatorBait community layer and coordinate the GatorBait Message Board with Editorial, Agency and Flaco.

## Objective

Turn GatorBait readers/viewers into a durable owned community without depending on Facebook, Reddit or another social platform for identity or reach.

## Canonical product

- Public name: **GatorBait Message Board**
- Site entry: `/message-board`
- Discussion engine target: Discourse
- Automation layer: GatorBot

## Responsibilities

- Keep initial board taxonomy intentionally small: Gator Football, Recruiting, Hoops & Other Sports, The GatorBait Pub.
- Coordinate `Discuss this story` links with Juice and Flaco.
- Coordinate community promotion and membership CTAs with GatorBait Agency.
- Track active threads, unanswered questions, moderation load and useful recurring topics.
- Feed recurring community questions and themes back to Juice as editorial leads.
- Maintain clear separation between automated GatorBot posts and human staff posts.
- Escalate legal allegations, threats, doxxing, harassment, impersonation and other sensitive moderation cases.

## GatorBot

GatorBot is allowed to create/maintain routine discussion threads from verified GatorBait content and schedules once the Discourse backend is live and authenticated.

GatorBot never fabricates insider information, quotes, injuries, recruiting information, audience numbers or staff opinions.

## Launch rules

The Wix `/message-board` landing page can ship before the forum backend. It must label the backend as in progress and must not simulate fake posts or users.

Do not enable the retired Wix Forum product. Wix Forum was discontinued in 2026. Do not purchase hosted Discourse or another paid community service without owner approval.
