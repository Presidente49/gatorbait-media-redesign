## Part 1: The Newsletter — "The Weekly Uncoupling"

### Newsletter Name: **The Weekly Uncoupling**

A weekly email to Divorce Club members and subscribers. Tongue-in-cheek, useful, and community-driven. Sent every Wednesday at 10 AM (mid-week pick-me-up).

### Newsletter Tool: **GHL Built-In Email** ($0 extra)

Since you already pay for GoHighLevel at $97/month, use its built-in email marketing. Zero additional cost. It handles subscriber management, segmentation, templates, automation, and analytics. If you ever outgrow it, upgrade to **Listmonk** (19,874 stars, self-hosted, $0) or **Plunk** (5,015 stars, open-source email platform).

### Newsletter Structure (Weekly Template)

**Subject Line Formula:** "[Emoji] + Provocative/Funny Hook"
Examples:
- "The one thing your divorce attorney won't tell you"
- "I ate cake in a parking lot and it changed my life"
- "Your ex is doing fine. Here's how to be better."

**Section 1: The Opening Shot** (2-3 sentences)
A personal anecdote or observation from Amanda or Brenden. Alternating weeks. Sets the tone — funny, real, relatable.

**Section 2: This Week at The Club** 
Upcoming events, podcast episode drop, community highlights. What's happening and how to get involved.

**Section 3: The Confessional**
One anonymous member-submitted story. The best one from the week. Community votes on their favorite.

**Section 4: One Thing That Actually Helps**
A single, actionable piece of advice. Not a listicle. One thing. Could be a book recommendation, a financial tip, a co-parenting hack, a self-care practice.

**Section 5: Merch Drop / Book Update / Podcast Clip**
Rotating promotional section. Never pushy. Always tied to content.

**Section 6: The Sign-Off**
A one-liner from Amanda or Brenden. Something that makes you smile before you close the email.

This Week: Private Dinner event, Episode 4 dropping, book chapter preview.

**Issue 5: "Your Friends Will Pick Sides"**
Opening: "Amanda here. I lost 6 friends in my divorce. Not because they died. Because they chose. And the ones who stayed? They're the ones who matter. If you're going through this right now, pay attention to who shows up. That's your real circle."
This Week: The Roast comedy night, Episode 5, founding member spotlight.
Confessional: "My best friend of 20 years told me she 'couldn't take sides.' She then went to my ex's birthday party. So I guess she can."

This Week: Workshop on dating profiles, Episode 6, merch restock.

Sign-Off: "Swipe right on yourself first. — Brenden"

**Issue 7: "The Money Chapter"**
Opening: "Amanda here. Let's talk about the thing nobody wants to talk about. Money. I went from a dual income household to figuring out how to pay for groceries and a mortgage on one salary. It's terrifying. It's also temporary. Here's what I wish someone had told me on day one."
