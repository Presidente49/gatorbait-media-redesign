Priorities · decisions · final verification

Muse organizes what has actually arrived: full copy, original headline, writer, images and credits. Missing manuscripts stay clearly marked as missing. A personal inbox does not establish access to the business inbox.

Codex confirms the actual writer and preserves the writer’s voice. Facts come from primary sources; quotes need their full source. Unresolved authorship or quote checks hold the story in private copy desk. Specialist agents return bounded evidence to the controller.

The controller publishes the ready story to native Wix Blog with the correct byline, category, alt text and photo credit. One canonical article avoids duplicate stories and duplicate alerts. Publication follows the active owner instructions.

An independent check reads the published page, images, author and links at phone and desktop widths. A material defect returns to the copy desk for a correction and another live check. A successful write response alone does not prove the page is right.

The primary Wix blog-email automation is active; its duplicate is off. The template is saved and its preview checked; delivery remains unverified. Wix follows its configured publication trigger. Template preview, actual send and verified delivery are distinct states.

Publish natively to the verified existing account when access and owner authorization are in place. Check for duplicates; retain the writer and photo credits. Confirm the real platform permalink before reporting a post as sent. Meta currently requires owner reconnection.

Match the story to a real Buddy Martin Show episode, interview, press conference or available clip. Verify the speaker, date and source. A relevant video is an editorial continuation; unrelated footage adds noise.

Use authorized native reporting and compare results at similar post ages. Track the evidence each platform provides. Missing account access means unknown results; it does not establish weak reach, success or a completed send.

Return useful reader questions, reporting gaps and measured outcomes to Codex and Muse. The controller chooses the next editorial action and records what changed. Learning feeds the next brief, without generating activity for its own sake.
