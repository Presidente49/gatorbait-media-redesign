## Structural findings

1. Existing taxonomy is rich enough to power the first Studio content feeds.
2. Categories mix four different concepts:
   - sport/topic
   - editorial priority
   - contributor
   - program/show
3. Therefore the new navigation must NOT mirror the raw category list.
4. Old category URLs should remain intact for compatibility while the new UI presents a smaller, clearer information architecture.
5. At least two recent posts currently have no category assignment and need editorial cleanup before cutover.
6. No live Blog mutations were made during this audit.
