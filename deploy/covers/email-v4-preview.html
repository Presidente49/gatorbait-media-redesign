THREE KEYS
1. Baugh against a top-20 run defense
Florida runs for 260 yards a game and has 18 rushing touchdowns, tied for most in the country.
2. Philo’s shots against a bend-don’t-break secondary
Aaron Philo’s 10.5 yards an attempt leads the SEC and he has been sacked twice in 104 throws.
3. Third downs and Austin Simmons
Florida allows 248 passing yards a game, 113th nationally, and opponents convert 41% on third down.
