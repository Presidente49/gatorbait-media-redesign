## THE BRAND

### Mission Statement

> The Divorce Club is a safe place to be broken. A place where it doesn't cost you $500,000 to get advice you could've gotten by having a drink with a friend. A place where you don't wear out your friends and family with your endless stories of custody battles and who gets who and what gets what. A community that's tongue-in-cheek, fun, and real — because the best therapy sometimes happens over a cocktail with someone who actually gets it.

### Brand Voice

The Divorce Club speaks like your funniest, most honest friend who's been through it. Not preachy. Not clinical. Not sad. Tongue-in-cheek, warm, occasionally dark-humored, always real. Think: the friend who makes you laugh about the worst thing that ever happened to you.

**Voice Rules:**
1. Never pity. Always empower.
2. Humor first, healing follows.
3. Inclusive — anyone divorced, any industry, any background.
4. Anonymous-friendly — people can be as open or private as they want.
5. No jargon. No therapy-speak. Real talk.

**Tagline:** *You Lost the Ring. You Found Your People.*

**Secondary Lines:**
- "New Chapter. New Circle. New Freedom."
- "Where your worst chapter becomes your best story."
- "Cheaper than therapy. Better than drinking alone."
- "The first rule of Divorce Club: tell everyone."

### The Man vs. Woman Perspective

Amanda and Brenden co-host everything with a **man vs. woman perspective on divorce**. This is the brand's secret weapon — two people, two genders, two completely different experiences of the same institution. They don't agree on everything. That's the point. It makes for great content, great debates, and gives every audience member someone to relate to.

        ↓
