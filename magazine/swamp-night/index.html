The Soothsayer P. 06

The Soothsayer likes the Gators in a white knuckler

ByFranz Beard

Desperate times call for desperate measures and in Norman, Oklahoma these are definitely desperate times.

That win three weeks ago over then No. 1 Texas only bought a short honeymoon which ended when Oklahoma (4-4, 2-3 SEC) got bitch-slapped into submission by South Carolina a week ago. Since then it has gone from honeymoon to hostility for head coach Brent Venables, who fired offensive coordinator Ben Arbuckle in an attempt to not only pacify boosters who want his head on a platter, but to salvage what's left of a season gone south.

When you find yourself strapped to a 40-gallon drum filled with high octane jet fuel and the boosters have an acetylene torch ready to make it go boom, you throw stuff against the wall hoping something will stick. That's what Venables did when he fired Arbuckle and named future Pro Football Hall of Fame tight end Jason Witten as his interim OC.
