## 3. Compliance for a media site in Florida

### 3.1 Florida rules

- **Sportsbook:** Hard Rock Bet only; **21+**. Sports betting advertising is governed by Seminole Tribal Gaming Commission rules under the 2021 compact; the compact requires all sportsbook advertising to carry a responsible-gambling message and a toll-free helpline. FGCC (Division of Pari-Mutuel Wagering) is the state compliance agency and can propose consumer-protection measures on advertising. [V flsenate.gov compact summary; [U] on exact ad-copy rules — the tribal regs are not public in searchable form]
- **Helpline language:** two numbers appear in Florida materials. **1-888-ADMIT-IT** (Florida Council on Compulsive Gambling, historically on all FL marketing) and **1-833-PLAYWISE (1-833-752-9947)**, quoted in the FGCC's Sept 14, 2026 football-season release. Use whichever the partner's IO mandates; when in doubt, show both. [V WDBO Sept 14, 2026 for PLAYWISE; [U] bettingusa for ADMIT-IT]
- **DFS / pick'em:** FGCC's Sept 2023 cease-and-desist letters to PrizePicks, Underdog, Betr forced an end to house pick'em (Feb/Mar 2024). Only peer-to-peer formats are offered in FL. Do not promote "pick'em vs the house" offers to Floridians. [V rotogrinders]
- **Prediction markets:** 18+, CFTC-regulated, no FL restriction today (Section 1.3). Copy should say "trading" / "event contracts," not "betting," and include the partner's risk language ("Trading is risky. 100% loss can occur.").
- **Age gating:** No Florida statute found that requires a site age-gate for affiliate content, but every partner IO will require 21+ (sportsbook) or 18+ (DFS/prediction) disclaimers on the creative. A simple "21+/18+ where applicable" banner on the betting zone plus RG line is the industry baseline. [U]

### 3.2 NCAA / UAA / SEC sensitivities

- **NCAA:** Athletes, coaches, and athletics staff cannot bet on any sport with an NCAA championship. UF's UAA compliance page restates this and lists compliance@gators.ufl.edu for violations. NCAA is actively lobbying CFTC and states against college prediction markets and college player props (~20 jurisdictions ban college props). [V floridagators.com; covers.com Jan 2026]
- **SEC:** Mandatory sports-gambling education for all athletes before first competition in 2026–27, IC360 ProhiBet monitoring, availability reports, anonymous tip line (Jan 2026). SEC says ~$12B was wagered on games involving SEC teams in 2025–26. [V covers.com May 27, 2026]
- **Federal:** Senate passed the Protect College Sports Act (S. 4668) 77–22 on Sept 28, 2026; Section 113(c) lets the NCAA/conferences restrict eligibility of athletes who take part in "sports wagering or sports-related event contracts" (i.e., Kalshi/Polymarket). House returns Nov 9. [V legalsportsbetting.com Sept 29, 2026]
- **Practical rules for GatorBait:** (a) feature team-outcome markets only, never individual Gators player props; (b) no content that could be read as tipping from insiders; (c) do not use NCAA marks ("March Madness") or imply UF/SEC endorsement — NCAA sent Kalshi a trademark demand; (d) keep the betting zone visually separate from the Buddy Martin editorial lead and the recruiting/injury coverage so nothing looks like inside information monetized; (e) credential sensitivity — a site with UAA media access should confirm UAA has no policy on credentialed outlets carrying sportsbook advertising before signing an IO (ask UF Athletics Communications; several schools have such policies). [U on UAA policy — not found publicly]

### 3.3 FTC affiliate disclosure

- 16 CFR Part 255 (Endorsement Guides, updated 2023). Disclosure must be **clear, conspicuous, and unavoidable**, placed **before/adjacent** to the link, not only in a footer or separate page. Applies to promo codes read aloud on The Buddy Martin Show too ("this segment is sponsored by…"). Operators can be held liable for affiliate non-disclosure, so partner IOs will enforce it. [V track360 FTC guide; qualityunit]
- Template line for the page and every article containing partner links: "GatorBait Media earns a commission if you sign up through links or codes on this page. 21+ (sportsbook) / 18+ (prediction markets & DFS). Gambling problem? Call 1-888-ADMIT-IT or 1-833-PLAYWISE."

### 3.4 Wix

- **Content Guidelines §9** prohibit "Illegal online casinos and gambling, sports betting, bingo, pyramid, and Ponzi schemes." The word **illegal** is the operative qualifier: editorial odds coverage and affiliate links to CFTC-regulated exchanges and the state-legal Hard Rock Bet are not on the prohibited list. [V wix.com/about/content-guidelines]
- **Wix Payments** prohibits processing payments for "legal gambling or games of skill" — irrelevant unless GatorBait tried to take wagers itself. Affiliate commissions are paid by the operator, not through Wix Payments. [V support.wix.com]
- Embeds: Wix allows third-party embeds via Add Elements > Embed Code > Embed a site (iframe). The Odds API documents this exact Wix path. [V]
- Residual risk [U]: Wix reserves the right to interpret guidelines "in its sole discretion." Keep the zone clearly editorial (odds, analysis, disclosures) rather than a bonus-code farm.

Reality check for a niche site with tens of thousands of monthly readers: affiliate income scales with **funded accounts**, not pageviews. A flat sponsorship (podcast read + page presence, fixed monthly fee) is usually more predictable than pure CPA at this size; ask Kalshi/PrizePicks for a hybrid (fixed fee + CPA) rather than CPA-only. No GatorBait number is estimated here on purpose.
