**Client:** Amanda Sarnes (The Marketing Mama) + Brenden Martin  
**Project:** The Divorce Club — Community, Events, Podcast, Book, Merch, Recruitment Funnel  
**Positioning:** SKOOL-level community for divorced individuals with indirect real estate professional recruitment  
**Timeline:** Soft Launch (Now) → Scale (6 months) → National Expansion (12 months)  
**Budget:** $97/month operational + event venue costs

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**The Divorce Club** is not a dating app. It's not a support group. It's not a therapist replacement.

It's a **members-only community** built on the principle that divorce is a transition, not a tragedy. A place where people can be real, get advice from peers who've been through it, attend events without judgment, and build their next chapter — together.

**The hidden layer:** Real estate professionals going through divorce become the natural gateway to recruiting agents into The Marketing Mama's agent network. They're already in transition. They're already thinking about their next move. They're already in the community. Conversion happens naturally through trust and community, not through sales pitches.

**The model:** SKOOL-level community infrastructure (private forum, live events, content hub) + grassroots real estate recruitment funnel + multiple revenue streams (membership, events, merch, book, podcast sponsorships) = a scalable, profitable community that runs autonomously.

## PART 1: BRAND POSITIONING & NARRATIVE

### Brand Name
**The Divorce Club**

### Tagline
**"You Lost the Ring. You Found Your People."**

### Mission Statement
To create a judgment-free, peer-led community where divorced individuals can share experiences, get real advice, attend events, and build their next chapter — without spending $500,000 on attorneys and therapists to figure it out.

### Core Values

1. **Real Over Polished** — No Instagram-filtered stories. No "I'm thriving" facades. Just honest conversations about custody battles, money stress, and what comes next.

2. **Peer-Led Over Expert-Driven** — The best advice comes from someone who's been through it. Experts have a place, but members are the heart.

3. **Community Over Transactional** — We're not here to sell you something. We're here to build something together. Revenue is a byproduct, not the purpose.

4. **Inclusive Over Exclusive** — Divorced teachers, realtors, entrepreneurs, stay-at-home parents, C-suite executives — everyone belongs. The realtor recruitment happens naturally, not through gatekeeping.

5. **Action Over Sympathy** — We listen. We validate. Then we help you move forward. This is a launchpad, not a waiting room.

### Brand Voice

- **Tone:** Irreverent, warm, direct, funny without being flippant
- **Language:** Conversational, no corporate jargon, permission to swear

**Example voice:**

### Visual Identity

- **Color Palette:** Deep charcoal/near-black backgrounds + hot magenta/neon pink accents + warm amber/gold highlights
- **Typography:** Bebas Neue (bold, dramatic headlines) + Space Grotesk (modern, readable body text)

## PART 3: REAL ESTATE RECRUITMENT FUNNEL

### The Hidden Layer: How Real Estate Agents Enter The Ecosystem

**Key Insight:** Real estate professionals going through divorce are already thinking about their next move. They're already in transition. They're already questioning everything. The Divorce Club becomes the natural entry point.

### Recruitment Flow

```
Real Estate Agent (Divorced) 
    ↓
Discovers The Divorce Club via geo-targeted ads or referral
    ↓
Joins free tier + attends first event
    ↓
Engages in community (forum, events, podcast)
    ↓
Becomes Club Member ($29/month)
    ↓
Attends "Real Estate Professionals" mastermind call (monthly)
    ↓
Meets other agents in transition
    ↓
Learns about The Marketing Mama's agent network
    ↓
Becomes a Marketing Mama agent (referral-based, no pressure)
    ↓
The Marketing Mama gets commission on agent's transactions
    ↓
Agent gets marketing support, lead generation, community
```

### Targeting Strategy

**Phase 1: Geo-Targeting (Months 1-3)**
- Target divorced individuals in real estate within 50 miles of your venue
- Facebook/Instagram ads: "Divorced Real Estate Agents: You're Not Alone"
- LinkedIn ads: "Real Estate Professionals Going Through Divorce"
- Google Search: Bid on "divorce + real estate" keywords in your market

**Phase 2: Event-Based (Months 3-6)**
- Host "Real Estate Professionals" monthly mastermind
- Invite local real estate groups, brokerage managers
- Partner with local brokerages for co-hosted events
- Offer free "Divorce & Real Estate" workshop at brokerage offices

**Phase 3: Referral Network (Months 6+)**
- Existing members refer other agents
- Attorneys, financial advisors, therapists refer clients
- The Marketing Mama agents refer their network
- Word-of-mouth becomes primary channel

### The Marketing Mama Integration

**How it works:**
1. Divorce Club member (real estate agent) attends "Real Estate Professionals" mastermind
2. Amanda or Brenden mention The Marketing Mama as a resource for agents in transition
3. No hard sell. Just: "If you're thinking about your next move professionally, Amanda's built something specifically for agents who want to scale without the corporate overhead."
4. Agent explores The Marketing Mama website (link in newsletter, forum, event materials)
5. Agent books a call with Amanda
6. If agent joins The Marketing Mama network, Amanda gets commission on their transactions
7. Agent gets marketing support, lead generation, and stays connected to The Divorce Club community

**Revenue Model for The Marketing Mama:**
- Commission per agent recruited: 10-20% of agent's first-year transaction volume (or flat $500-1000 per agent)
- Upsell: Marketing services, social media management, lead generation
- Network effect: More agents → more referrals → more community growth

**Why This Works:**
- Agents are already in transition (open to new opportunities)
- They've already built trust with The Divorce Club
- They're surrounded by peers also exploring their options
- No pressure. It's organic. It's peer-led.

## PART 10: RISK MITIGATION & CONTINGENCIES

### Risk 1: Low Initial Adoption
**Mitigation:**
- Heavy email outreach to Amanda's existing network
- Influencer partnerships (divorce coaches, therapists, attorneys)
- Heavy discounting for first 100 members
- Referral incentives

### Risk 2: Low Real Estate Professional Conversion
**Mitigation:**
- Dedicated "Real Estate Professionals" mastermind
- Direct outreach to local brokerages
- Partner with real estate associations
- Offer free trial membership to agents

### Risk 3: Event Venue Costs Exceed Revenue
**Mitigation:**
- Start with virtual events (zero cost)
- Partner with local businesses for free/discounted venues
- Increase ticket prices for in-person events
- Sponsor-funded events (local businesses pay for venue in exchange for branding)

### Risk 4: Podcast Doesn't Gain Traction
**Mitigation:**
- Guest appearances on other podcasts (cross-promotion)
- Repurpose podcast content for social media, blog, newsletter
- Podcast sponsorships (even with small audience, sponsors still interested)
- Pivot to video content (YouTube, TikTok)

### Risk 5: Merch Sales Underperform
**Mitigation:**
- Printify handles fulfillment (zero inventory risk)
- Design limited-edition drops (scarcity)
- Member-exclusive merch (drives membership)
- Merch as event giveaways
