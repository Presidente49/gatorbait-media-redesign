This space represents the reading experience. The invitation is a small nonmodal banner. It waits for time and engagement, stays out of the way of forms and dialogs, and can be closed with its button or Escape.

Opening a signup form is recorded as a choice to open it. A successful submission is pending confirmation. This browser cannot prove whether the reader confirmed an email or is already subscribed on another device.

The unchanged Wix form, consent checkbox and anonymous submission client remain the source of the signup. Actual confirmation delivery and source attribution remain separate release checks.
