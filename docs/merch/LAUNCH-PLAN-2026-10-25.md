## 6. Risks

**Trademark.** University of Florida marks (the Gator head, "Gators," "Florida Gators," the F, orange-and-blue trade dress on licensed goods) are licensed through CLC; we use none of them. Our marks are GatorBait's own script, the Gainesville wordmark and the GB monogram, all in Barlow. USPTO search for "GATOR BAIT" (read Oct. 25, 2026, first 25 of 2,321 hits): no live Class 025 (apparel) registration. The one apparel registration (Steve Varasteh Inc., reg. 2640073) was cancelled in 2009. Live marks are for golf balls, gaming machines and a pending beer filing; "BAIT" alone is registered in Class 025 by Bait Inc. We found **no GatorBait Media registration** in those hits; a filing in Class 025 and 041 is worth a lawyer's hour. Separate sensitivity: UF retired the "Gator Bait" cheer in 2020 over its racist history and revived it in Sept. 2026 (ESPN, Washington Post). The brand is 47 years old and the name is ours, but copy should never reference the chant.

**Sales tax.** Printful is not our seller of record on a Wix store; it charges **us** tax in states where it has nexus unless we file a resale certificate with Printful, and **we** must collect from buyers where we have nexus (Florida, at minimum). Wix Stores has automatic tax calculation through Avalara on eligible plans; remitting to Florida is still ours. Fourthwall is the one option that acts as merchant of record and remits for us. Shopify Tax calculates but does not remit. Decision for Brenden's accountant before launch.

**Returns.** Printful replaces misprints and damage reported within 30 days; wrong-size returns are on us. Policy to post: free replacement for defects, exchanges for size at the buyer's shipping cost, no returns on worn goods. Baker's current "no refunds or exchanges" line stays on their store only.

**Other.** Sample and print color on navy must be checked in daylight; the Growth plan is a trap at low volume; YPP watch hours are unverified; one production writer only; no live mutation until items 1–3 are answered.
