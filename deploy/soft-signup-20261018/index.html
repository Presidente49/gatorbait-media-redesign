Soft email signup for story pages

One small, dismissible strip after the third paragraph replaces the signup slide-up. It never blocks reading. It has no email box: it sends the reader to a Wix form that uses email confirmation.

The safest working method

A native Wix Forms form on its own page, with double opt-in on. Our strip only links to it. Our code never creates a contact and never calls a Wix API.

  • Verified (read-only, today): form GatorBait Email List (6babfee8) is new Wix Forms, subscribe checkbox is required and unchecked by default, opt-in level DOUBLE_CONFIRMATION on EMAIL, spam filter ADVANCED. No Velo needed: it is the Subscribe field's "Enable double opt-in" toggle (Wix Help Center, updated Oct. 5), already on.
  • What email confirmation does: the contact is created with status Subscription Pending (API value PENDING) and Wix emails a "Confirm Subscription" link. The contact becomes SUBSCRIBED only after the click. No expiry.
  • How an unconfirmed contact is kept out of sends: by status. Pending is not SUBSCRIBED. Our audience gate (SUBSCRIBED + VALID or NOT_SET, no hold or Do Not Market, de-duplicated) already excludes it, and START-HERE section 7 already requires it. One gap to close: automation fea566c1 adds the audience labels at submission, so a pending contact would carry them. I could not verify from Wix docs that a label-targeted send skips Pending contacts. Do not rely on the label; rely on the status gate, and give the new form its own automation that adds only a source label (step 2).
  • Source label: the public Forms schema has no label setting on the form, so the label comes from a native user automation (addLabelsToContact, the same pattern as fea566c1) on a duplicate form: Source: Story Prompt.

What this cannot do

  • No signup inside the strip: one extra tap to the form page.
  • It does not close the old API path. Removing the UI stops our code from using it; the form stays reachable by anyone with the public client ID. The form's spam filter and double opt-in are the only gates. Whether the ADVANCED filter stops script submissions is unproven (earlier note in deploy/capture-2026/README.md, item c).
  • Member hiding is a browser guess (no Velo). See below.
  • One label per form. The homepage hub module uses form 6babfee8 and keeps its own behaviour.

Member hiding

  • Signal: the same one the live shell already uses. If the header's login button ([data-testid="handle-button"], .login-social-bar button) shows anything other than log in, sign in or join, the reader is treated as signed in and the strip never renders.
  • Also hidden: readers who signed up in this browser (gbm-capture-joined), who dismissed in the last 14 days, on posts under 5 real paragraphs, and anyone who already scrolled past the third paragraph.
  • Limit: a member whose header has not drawn within the page's first moments, or a shell change, can still see it. The cost is one dismissible strip. Not proven on a real member session: I have no member login. Check it once with Brenden's account.
