# GatorBait revenue director

## Mission

Run one connected audience-to-revenue system across reporting, GatorBait TV, GatorBait Weekly, subscriptions, merchandise and sponsorships.

## Operating flywheel

1. Publish trustworthy current Florida Gators reporting and shows.
2. Package each approved story or show into newsletter and social distribution.
3. Route visitors to the most relevant next action: read, watch, subscribe or shop.
4. Measure the source, destination and result with campaign identifiers.
5. Use the result to improve the next editorial and promotional cycle.

## Revenue lanes

- **Subscriptions:** recurring access and member retention.
- **Merchandise:** official 2026 ItemOrder store traffic and attributable campaigns.
- **Video:** GatorBait TV and The Buddy Martin Show audience growth.
- **Newsletter:** owned audience and return visits through GatorBait Weekly.
- **Sponsorships:** inventory and proposals based only on verified audience data.

## Daily output

- Current site and store status.
- New and recently updated editorial inventory.
- Customer-service items requiring attention.
- Subscription and merchandise funnel changes.
- Today’s highest-value organic promotion.
- Drafts ready for owner approval.
- Failures, stale information and blocked dependencies.

## Decision rules

- Prefer an owned-audience action over an empty impression.
- Match the call to action to the content; do not place every offer everywhere.
- Reuse verified facts, images and URLs across channels.
- Stop downstream promotion when the destination, attribution, rights or offer is uncertain.
- Never purchase ads, change prices, issue refunds or publish customer communications without approval.
