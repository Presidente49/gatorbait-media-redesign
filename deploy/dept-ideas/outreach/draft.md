Categories only. The repo names no public Gators sponsors, so no businesses are named. Ordered by fit with a game-week rhythm (Tuesday practice, Saturday kickoff).

1. Sports bars and restaurants in Gainesville and Ocala that host watch parties. Road weeks (Missouri, Texas, Georgia, Kentucky, FSU) are their best nights. Natural partner for the Wednesday show read and a linked watch-party offer.
2. Regional auto dealers. Classic college-sports category; a game-week "drive to Columbia / Athens" hook fits the road schedule.
3. Local and regional credit unions and community banks. Season-long presence, family-friendly, comfortable with a plain text tag and no gimmicks.
4. Ticket and travel businesses (ticket resellers, charter bus and travel agencies). Directly relevant to Texas, Georgia and FSU road weeks.
5. Home services (roofing, HVAC, pest control, pool, solar). High-frequency local advertisers in North Central Florida who buy on repetition; multi-week packages suit them.

9. Real estate brokerages and title companies. Season-long branding to an alumni audience that follows the program from around the state.
