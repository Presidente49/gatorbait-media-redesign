# Loop: newsroom

**Goal:** every writer's filing is live, clean and correctly bylined within an hour of arrival during desk hours, with no duplicate posts or alerts.

**Owner:** Jarvis, through the Newsroom desk routine (`trig_01GseZnxrzHCebbuHJ1LFqxN`, hourly 6:20 a.m. to 11:20 p.m. ET). The job spec is `docs/jobs/newsroom-desk.md`; this file is the loop's memory, not a second spec.

**Scoreboard:** minutes from filing to live; copy-desk defects found after publish; stories held, and why.

## Each turn

1. **OBSERVE.** Check Gmail for writer filings since the last turn (Buddy Martin, Franz Beard, Eddie Gilley, Loren Meadows, Carlton Reese). Check Wix for posts published or edited since the last turn, then check both blog-alert automations are INACTIVE.
2. **SCOPE.** Pick, in order: new filings not yet live, then live posts missing a cover, alt text, category or SEO description, then everything else.
3. **CLASSIFY.** A writer piece gets copy-desk fixes only. Facts that can't be sourced are held and flagged, never guessed.
4. **ACT and VERIFY.** Publish under the writer's own member, read the post back, and open the public URL.
5. **RECORD.** One line in #34 per shipped item. Silence when nothing changed.

## Known blockers and workarounds

- **Word attachments can't be read through the Gmail connector.** Ask the writer to paste the text into the email body, or have Brenden drop the file in Drive. Brenden's Oct. 15 reply template already asks writers for plain photos as separate attachments.
- **Facts in a column that outrun the reporting** (for example, "won't start" when the reporting says "status uncertain"): soften to what the source supports, or hold. Never publish the stronger claim.
- **Another session or Brenden may publish the same piece.** Re-query Wix right before creating anything.

## Lessons
