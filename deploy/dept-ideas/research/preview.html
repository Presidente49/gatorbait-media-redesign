How it stays current

The feed already refreshes on a five-minute tick from ESPN's public site API. Once live, The Ledger reads the same three endpoints: the Florida schedule (teams/57/schedule), the SEC standings (standings?group=8) and the game summary (summary?event=ID) for every final, so the quarter strip covers all 12 games, not just the last one.

Source: automation/scoreboard_feed.py and .github/workflows/refresh-newsroom-feed.yml in this repo. No new scheduler, no paid fetches.
