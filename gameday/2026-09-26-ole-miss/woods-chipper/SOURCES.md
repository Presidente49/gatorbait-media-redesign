## Discrepancies and notes

1. **Transcript 171204 label.** The brief said the page was mislabeled "Jaden Edgecombe." The live fetch (ttl 0) on Sept. 27 labels the speaker **JAYDEN WOODS** throughout, so the page appears to have been corrected. Content matches the brief (strip-sack walkthrough, "blacked out," "bend but don't break"). Attributed to Woods.
2. **Stockton punt return yardage.** ESPN PBP text says 25 yards to the OLE44. ESPN box credits 1 return for **16** yards, which matches the spot of the holding foul (FLA47, from the catch at FLA31). The article uses the PBP description ("returned ... 25 yards to the Ole Miss 44", then the penalty) and does not cite 16. The editor can switch to "16-yard return" if the box figure is preferred.
3. **Stockton injury play.** Sumrall said Stockton got hurt "on the shot, on the targeting call." The only targeting call in the PBP (L90, Q2 3:44, on #6 J. Aguero) lists #4 T. Abrams as the receiver, not Stockton. Stockton may have been hit elsewhere on the play. The article says only that Stockton took the shot in the first half and does not tie it to the targeting call.
4. **Woods tackle stats.** The box's FLA defensive list (top 4 only) does not include Woods, and ESPN's box shows no individual sack totals for the listed players. The strip-sack credit relies on PBP L165 (#0 J.Woods). Woods also made a tackle for a 5-yard loss on Q2 11:20 (PBP L72), which the article does not use.
5. **Field-position context left out.** Clark's 43-yard first-half punt (PBP L67) drew a kick-catch interference penalty on L. Hayward that gave Ole Miss the ball at the FLA 48, and that drive became a Carneiro field goal. The article does not mention it.
6. **Coleman INT timing.** The interception came with Florida already up 52-28 (2:36 left). The article frames it as the "last word," not as a game-saving play.
7. **"31 points" and "0-for-5"** are GatorBait calculations from the PBP, not ESPN-published figures. The derivations are shown above.
