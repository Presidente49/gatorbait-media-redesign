# Recruiting Desk

Owner direction, Nov. 16, 2026 (Brenden Martin): GatorBait publishes recruiting coverage regularly, with its own section on the site. This file is the protocol for whoever writes it. It sits under [the writer skill](../SKILL.md); the hard rules there still apply except where this file says the owner changed them for this lane.

## What the lane covers

- Football recruiting: commitments, flips, decommitments, offers that matter, visit lists, signing periods.
- Basketball recruiting, men's and women's.
- The transfer portal: Florida players entering, players committing to Florida.
- NIL: deals, collectives, rule changes and rulings that touch Florida.

## Where to look

Look widely every time. Read what loads; do not fight a block or a login wall.

1. **The player's own announcement** on social media (X, Instagram). This is the primary source for a commitment, flip, decommitment or portal entry.
2. **Florida's official accounts and FloridaGators.com** for signings, enrollments and roster moves. A school cannot comment on an unsigned recruit, so silence there means nothing.
3. **ESPN:** team news feeds for football and basketball, recruiting and portal coverage.
4. **On3 and 247Sports,** then Rivals: commitment lists, team rankings, portal trackers and their reporters' posts.
5. **Beat reporters and national recruiting reporters on social media.** These are tips until a primary source or a second named outlet confirms them.
6. A general news search for the last day.

Never use, name, quote or link Gator Country.

## Sourcing rule (changed by the owner for this lane)

For game statistics the rule is still ESPN or FloridaGators.com only. For recruiting, the owner approved crediting recruiting services by name:

- Every fact is credited in the story to a named source: the player's own announcement, the school, ESPN, On3, 247Sports or Rivals. "According to On3" is the form.
- A ranking always names the service and the date it was read. Services disagree; where two were read, give both.
- A commitment, flip, decommitment or portal move runs when the player has announced it, or when two named outlets report it. One outlet alone runs only if the story says it is that outlet's report and is not yet confirmed.
- Nothing goes in that the writer did not read that day. No memory, no "sources say", no invented quotes.
- A recruit's quote comes only from his or her own public post or a video the writer watched. Quote a short phrase at most from another outlet's interview, with credit.
- Recruits are often minors. Report football, not private life: no home addresses, family finances, grades or health details beyond what the player or school published.

## Writing

- GatorBait's own words, always. Match how often the recruiting outlets post and what they cover; never copy or closely paraphrase their wording.
- AP style. Short. Lead with what happened and who he is: name, position, high school, city, height and weight, the ranking with its service.
- Say what it means for the class: position need, class rank, who else is committed there.
- Verbal commitments are not binding until a player signs. Say so when it matters.

## Story types and pace

- A short news story for each commitment, flip, decommitment, portal move or NIL development, the day it happens.
- A recruiting notebook around each home game (who is visiting, where the class stands).
- A class update when the rankings move.
- The scheduled morning check ("GatorBait morning recruiting check") looks every day and publishes up to three stories; on a quiet day it publishes nothing.

## Publishing

- Byline: GatorBait Staff, unless Brenden names a writer.
- Categories: Gator Recruiting, plus Gator Football or Gator Basketball.
- **Never the front-page lead.** The homepage leads with the newest post, so set the recruiting post's publish time a few minutes behind the current newest non-recruiting post.
- Art: the "Recruiting Notebook" series card, credited "Graphic: GatorBait Media". No recruit photos without a clear, permitted source and a credit.
- Receipt on issue #34 after each publish: what ran and which sources it rests on.
- No subscriber email, no automation change.
