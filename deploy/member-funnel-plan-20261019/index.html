Small, verifiable rollout

Prepare first. Prove each step.

Prepare the paused workflows

The welcome opt-out safeguard is saved with the workflow still inactive. The three-day expiry candidate validated, but Wix blocked its locked trigger change. Keep the active blog route, confirmation and payment recovery. Paused does not mean broken.

Install the native member experience

Inspect existing pages first. The Mac Wix Editor session is the documented integration path. Review the exact page, navigation and permission batch before any separately authorized site publish.

Verify the complete journey

With owner-authorized real tests: confirmation receipt/click, confirmed state, correct welcome recipient and later genuine story delivery. Check web and Spaces access, phone behavior and unsubscribe. No fabricated post or test blast.

Release only the agreed pieces

Brenden approves each activation, subscriber send or schedule, Spaces announcement and membership/access change. No price, charge, account connection or blanket campaign authority follows from this plan.

Confirmed readers

Confirmed signups ÷ eligible form submissions. Separate pending and unresolved records.

Useful delivery

Actual delivered emails, clicks, opt-outs, bounces and complaints. An active workflow is not delivery.

Member starts

Distinct trial starts, first paid starts and current entitlement. Separate each from email engagement.

Paid renewals

Linked, positive-value approved payment transactions at the right cycle, accounting for refunds and cancellations.

Success means verified reader access, useful delivery and actual paid retention. Newsletter clicks, a “plan ordered” event or a payment-recovered notification alone do not prove cash receipts. The recovered-payment trigger has no transaction ID or payment amount.
