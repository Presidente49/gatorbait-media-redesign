# Loop: newsroom to distribution

Goal: every genuine writer filing reaches a verified canonical story and appropriate distribution, without owner micromanagement or fabricated status.

## One controller and explicit acceptance

Codex owns the queue and is the sole production writer. Muse receives source copy, prepares visuals/native social and evaluates engagement; the copy desk and fact reviewer return concrete evidence. A handoff is accepted only when the receiving stage acknowledges the exact item, source/revision, next action and checks. Posting a pointer alone does not complete a handoff.
