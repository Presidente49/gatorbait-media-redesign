# Writing pitch: The Front Porch with Buddy Martin

## 1) The idea (two sentences)

A weekly Magazine column in which Buddy Martin walks the reader through the whole GatorBait week in one sitting, hanging each stop on one already-published staff story by its canonical URL and closing with a single look at Saturday. It is the curated multi-story package Brenden asked for, written in the voice of the editorial lead, with no story republished and no second URL created.

## 2) Why readers or revenue care

- Readers get the week in 800 words with the receipts, and the column pushes them into five to eight staff stories (19 GatorBait links in the first issue) instead of one. That is deeper sessions on the free homepage, where the ad inventory lives.
- Every issue is a Magazine cover story that still reads well a week later, which is the Magazine's stated job, and it gives the paid tier a reason that repeats every Tuesday.
- It is ready-made newsletter copy. The Sept. 23 GatorBait Magazine Newsletter edition was assembled from three Buddy columns plus Thoughts of the Day; the Porch is that edition written as one piece, by the sender the list already knows, with links back to the site rather than full text in the email. Any send stays a separate, owner-authorized decision.
- It puts every byline on the staff in front of Buddy's audience by name each week (Franz, Eddie, Loren, Chris, Brenden, Staff), which is cheap morale and cheap SEO internal linking at once.
- Cost is near zero: no new module, feed, design system or tool. Words on the existing Wix Blog, existing Magazine cover slot, existing category.

## 3) How it is built (cadence, who writes, hours per issue)

- Cadence: Tuesday morning during football season, after Monday's Sumrall press-conference coverage posts; every other week in the offseason for basketball, baseball, recruiting and the legal beat.
- Who writes: Buddy writes or dictates. A staffer (Brenden or GatorBait Staff) preps a one-page link sheet Monday night: the week's stories newest-first, one line each, plus the four Ledger numbers from the ESPN scoreboard feed already in the repo (`sports-live/scoreboard.json`). Buddy writes from that sheet.
- Copy desk: one pass for AP style, link check and number check against the linked stories. No quotes unless they appear in a linked story.
- Hours per issue: about 45 minutes for the link sheet, 90 minutes for Buddy, 30 minutes for edit and Magazine placement. Roughly 2.75 hours all in, against a first issue that already exists in `deploy/dept-ideas/writing/draft.md` and `preview.html`.
- Placement: one canonical URL, Magazine as primary home, homepage lists it newest-first like any other story. Follows `docs/HOMEPAGE-BASELINE-LOCK.md` and the "one primary editorial home" rule; nothing about the current homepage presentation changes.

## 4) What it needs from Brenden

- Buddy's yes on the name and the shape, and his edit of the first issue (email for approval per house rule; nothing is sent from this draft).
- A standing Tuesday slot on the Magazine cover and a category decision (existing Buddy's Blog tag suggested; no new category).
- A named staffer for the Monday-night link sheet.
- Sign-off that the column may be used as a newsletter body when he authorizes an edition; this pitch does not assume any send.
- Re-verification of three inherited facts before publishing (first 4-0 since 2019, three of the last four trips to Columbia, FPI 10-2), all taken from Buddy's own published columns because this draft was built read-only without network access.

## 5) Risks

- Voice drift: if a staffer drafts it in a hurry, it reads like a link roundup, not Buddy. Mitigation: Buddy writes or dictates; the staffer only preps the sheet.
- Redundancy with Thoughts of the Day and Buddy's game-night columns. Mitigation: the Porch never re-runs a paragraph; it points to those pieces and adds the through-line. If Buddy's Saturday-night column and the Porch feel like the same piece two days apart, fold his Saturday take into the Porch and keep one.
- Stale facts: the column inherits every number from the linked stories and the ESPN feed. A wrong link or corrected story propagates. Mitigation: link check and number check in the copy pass; corrections posted to the linked story, not silently to the Porch.
- Schedule slip in bye weeks or off-weeks with little news. Mitigation: shape holds at three stops; skip rather than pad.
- Reader fatigue if it becomes a chore. Mitigation: the Porch Light closer must be one paragraph and must look forward only; the Ledger strip stays at four numbers.
