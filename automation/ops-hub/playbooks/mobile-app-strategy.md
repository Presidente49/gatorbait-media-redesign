# GatorBait Mobile App Strategy Playbook

## Objective

Decide deliberately whether GatorBait should use Spaces by Wix, stay mobile-web-first, or publish a fully branded native iOS/Android app. Do not pay for or launch a branded app until a free/low-cost pilot proves the audience behavior we need.

## Current Wix paths

### Spaces by Wix — pilot layer

Spaces is Wix's free member app. It can give GatorBait members mobile access to site/blog content, notifications, member activity, paid-plan activity, store/community functions and other Wix-connected experiences.

Use Spaces first as a behavioral prototype because it does not require building or publishing a separate branded app.

### Wix native app builder — branded layer

Wix also supports building a separate native iOS/Android app with GatorBait branding and no Wix branding. Publishing that app requires a Mobile App Premium plan plus separate Apple/Google developer-account costs.

A branded app should be treated as a product investment, not a vanity project.

## Phase 1 — Spaces pilot

Configure the GatorBait member experience around the jobs that matter to a sports publisher:

1. **Home / Latest** — current Florida coverage.
2. **Magazine** — current issue and best features.
3. **GatorBait TV** — shows, recap posts and owned video destinations.
4. **Membership** — plan status and clear member benefits.
5. **Store** — official merchandise destination when useful.
6. **Notifications** — breaking news, live-show reminders and major new coverage only.

Do not add chat/community clutter merely because the platform supports it. Every tab must have a real editorial or business job.

## Pilot audience

Start with a bounded invited cohort rather than the whole email list. Existing members/readers who already use GatorBait frequently are the best initial testers.

Track:

- invitations sent;
- join/install rate;
- push-notification opt-in rate;
- weekly active users among joined users;
- article/show opens from mobile notifications;
- repeat sessions;
- membership-page visits or renewals attributable to the mobile experience;
- complaints, notification disables or abandonment.

No single metric decides the outcome. The controller compares app behavior against mobile web/email/social behavior.

## Decision gate for a branded native app

Recommend paying for the branded native app only when the Spaces/mobile pilot demonstrates several of these signals for at least a few weeks:

- a meaningful share of invited loyal users actually joins and returns;
- push notifications reliably drive qualified visits without high opt-out rates;
- mobile users consume multiple articles/shows per week rather than opening once and disappearing;
- mobile engagement or retention is materially better than the equivalent mobile-web path;
- the app has at least one durable job that the website/social/email combination does not handle as well;
- expected membership, sponsorship or advertising value can reasonably justify the recurring app cost and store/developer fees.

If those signals do not appear, keep improving the mobile website and use Spaces selectively. Do not build a branded app simply to say GatorBait has one.

## Branded app product shape if the gate passes

The first version should be intentionally narrow:

- latest news/home feed;
- GatorBait Magazine/issues;
- GatorBait TV and show recap/clips;
- push alerts with user-controlled categories;
- membership/login/account state;
- official store link or native commerce only if it adds value;
- saved/favorite content if the Wix product supports it reliably.

Avoid launching with a giant community/forum feature set unless users have demonstrated demand.

## Notification policy

Push notifications are the main strategic advantage of an app and also the fastest way to make users uninstall it.

Default categories:

- breaking Florida/Gators news;
- live-show starting soon;
- major postgame/analysis package;
- optional Magazine/current-issue alert.

Do not push every story or every social clip. A user should be able to control categories where Wix/app capabilities allow it.

## Desktop/mobile parity principle

The app does not excuse weak mobile web. The website remains the canonical public product for search, links and casual readers. App content should resolve from the same verified editorial sources whenever possible.

The controller should treat these as one content system with multiple surfaces:

`Wix canonical content → web desktop/mobile → Spaces/native app → newsletter/social/video`

Do not create separate duplicate editorial truth inside the app.

## App QA

Before inviting a broader pilot cohort, verify on both iOS and Android where possible:

- navigation order and labels;
- article readability;
- images/video fit the viewport;
- login and pricing-plan state;
- notification deep links land on the correct content;
- no dead tabs or empty screens;
- accessibility and text scaling remain usable;
- store/external links open safely;
- analytics can distinguish app-origin traffic where available.

## Stop conditions

Stop or hold expansion when:

- users cannot reliably find current content;
- notification deep links fail or land on stale content;
- app state disagrees with current membership/access state;
- adoption remains weak after a representative test;
- a paid plan or app-store commitment would be made without owner approval;
- the app requires duplicating editorial workflows that should remain canonical in Wix.

## Research basis — September 2026

- Wix describes Spaces by Wix as a free member app for blog/content, store, notifications, member activity and community interaction.
- Wix offers a separate native app builder for branded iOS/Android apps with no Wix branding.
- Publishing a Wix-built native app requires a Mobile App Premium plan.
- Wix states Apple App Store developer membership starts at $99/year and Google Play registration is a $25 one-time fee; these fees are separate from the Wix mobile-app plan.
