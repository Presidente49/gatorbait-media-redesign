GAINESVILLE — Jadan Baugh has two touchdowns, the Florida defense has a takeaway and No. 21 Florida leads No. 4 Ole Miss 17-3 at halftime in The Swamp.

Baugh scored on a 4-yard run on the opening drive and a 2-yard run in the second quarter. Patrick Durkin added a 49-yard field goal, and Aaron Chiles forced a fumble from Ole Miss running back JT Lindsey, who is filling in for the injured Kewan Lacy.

Ole Miss, playing without Lacy and nine other players ruled out before kickoff, got only a 36-yard Lucas Carneiro field goal. That drive started at the Florida 48 after a pass-interference penalty.

Florida's lead could have been bigger. An Aaron Philo touchdown pass to Vernell Brown III was wiped out by a holding penalty early in the second quarter.
