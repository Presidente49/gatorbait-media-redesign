# Pregame edition, Nov. 21, 2026 (Vanderbilt at Florida)

Senior Day at Ben Hill Griffin Stadium, noon ET on SEC Network (ESPN event 401856748). Built Nov. 20. Local build only: nothing here writes to Wix, sends mail or pushes.

- **Layout:** `layout: "pregame"` (the Kentucky edition's renderer and skin). Franz Beard is at the top: the cover and the lead column are his Nov. 20 Soothsayer, the Florida-Vanderbilt section in full and word for word, the rest linked to the one canonical post. Then the live kickoff countdown and game card (ESPN line, total, moneyline, matchup predictor, forecast), the series since 2012, six numbers, **the schedules**, a 10-row tale of the tape, the column, the Sooth Board, eight story cards, **the rosters** and the reference desk.
- **Pregame standards (owner rule, Nov. 20):** "rosters for both teams up and visible, and a schedule up and visible; these are standards." Two new sections, `teamSchedules` and `teamRosters` in the issue JSON, rendered by `sports-live/src/magazine-pregame-standards.{js,css}`. Both are open tables (no tap, no `<details>`): Florida's full 2026 schedule and Vanderbilt's with every result so far, and both ESPN rosters grouped offense, defense and special teams (number, name, position, class, height, weight, hometown). A jump strip under the cover links to them. Rows are stored as arrays to protect the 184 KB cap.
- **Opt-in by data:** `sports-live/build-magazine.mjs` bundles the standards renderer and skin, and hooks them into `renderPregame`, only when the issue carries `teamSchedules` or `teamRosters`. `sports-live/src/magazine-pregame.{js,css}` are untouched, so every earlier edition builds to the same bytes as before. To give a future pregame edition the standards, add the two keys to its issue JSON (shapes are in the header of `magazine-pregame-standards.js`).
- **Build:** `python3 deploy/magazine-2026/pregame-vanderbilt/build_issue.py` (fetches ESPN: event summary and pickcenter, rankings, team statistics, schedules 2012-2026, both rosters and Saturday's SEC scoreboard; checks every quoted line of the column against the public post) writes `sports-live/magazine-issue-vanderbilt.json`. Then `MAG_VARIANT=vanderbilt node sports-live/build-magazine.mjs` writes `sports-live/magazine-vanderbilt.js` and the QA fixture `sports-live/magazine-vanderbilt-frame.html`. No esbuild needed.
- **QA:** `node sports-live/qa-magazine-vanderbilt.mjs <outdir>` at 320/390/430/1366: sections, both standards (present, height, open, every team and row from the issue JSON, nothing wider than its panel), overflow, fonts, photos, links and the console, with screenshots.
- **Refresh before release:** run the build again on game morning. The line, total, forecast, records, rankings and rosters are read from ESPN at build time and the script stops if the game, the SEC slate or the column changed. If ESPN starts listing injuries the script says so; add the availability block then.
- **Left out on purpose:** the keys block (no "Keys to the Win" piece for this game on the site; the Nov. 16 one grades the Kentucky game), the availability block (ESPN lists no injuries and no GatorBait story this week reports any for this game), the photo mosaic, and Tennessee Tech at Mississippi State on the Sooth Board (the column names no winner for it).
- **Photos:** the cover is the owner's pick, Wix media `d3cfa5_9d90bd2c81974bf39a13495fc5b8eaf9~mv2.png` (Florida's sideline at Kentucky, Nov. 14), credited "Photo courtesy UAA (University Athletic Association, FloridaGators.com)"; its alt text names no player. Three cards run their story's own credited art. Five run as text cards: two recruiting stories whose covers are Oklahoma-game file photos, Buddy Martin's Nov. 17 column (its cover looks like the Sept. 26 Ole Miss game), the Philo video-still story and the Thoughts of the Day (a basketball photo). Every photo is a distinct file. See docs/PHOTO-ATTRIBUTION.md.
