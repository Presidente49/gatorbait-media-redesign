# GatorBait Studio Lab Design System

## Product
A modern Florida Gators journalism, video, magazine and community product. Journalism is primary. Multimedia and community deepen engagement.

## Visual hierarchy
News = restrained and fast.
TV = cinematic navy surfaces and strong media hierarchy.
Magazine = immersive photography, editorial serif, richer motion.
Community = dense, readable and functional.

## Typography
Display: Georgia/serif reference until Studio brand type is finalized.
UI/body: system sans.
Headlines: tight leading, restrained tracking.
Never oversized on mobile.

## Motion
Use reveal, progress and rail interactions.
Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
No scroll-jacking.
No first-paint hiding.
No decorative continuous animation in News.
