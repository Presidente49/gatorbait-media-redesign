Implementation-ready proposal

GatorBait weekly magazine and membership specification

Candidate for business approval and implementation planning. October 15, 2026 UTC / October 14 ET. This document does not change prices, billing, memberships, entitlements, consent, automation or publication settings.

Product decision

Use one editorial product with two clear ways to participate:

  • Free Friday newsletter: a useful, concise weekly email with selected headlines, a short editor introduction, links to free reporting, and the current shows. Its primary conversion destination is the complete weekly magazine. A visitor can subscribe to this email without purchasing a plan.
  • Paid complete weekly magazine: the full issue in a mobile reading edition, a downloadable edition for offline reading, and the member issue archive. Monthly and annual payment options receive the same stated magazine benefits. Annual billing changes the commitment and billing interval, not the completeness of the issue.

Preserve existing members, plan terms, complimentary access, article restrictions and All Access entitlements. Do not migrate subscriptions, remove benefits or reinterpret an existing All Access promise to launch the magazine. Describe any broader All Access benefits separately once their current definition is verified. Existing open articles remain open.

Root's current plan evidence is $9.99 monthly, $99 annually and a seven-day trial. These are existing terms, not a proposed financial change. The offer documented for GATORNIGHT20 is 20% off the first annual payment followed by standard renewal; current eligibility, expiration and checkout calculation must be verified before further promotion. Do not represent the discount as permanent.

Weekly editorial and production contract

Publish one clearly dated issue per week, with the Friday newsletter pointing to that issue. State the actual editorial cutoff and publication time after the team confirms capacity; no delivery time is invented here. Live web stories can update independently, while the downloadable issue retains its issue date and a visible correction/version notice when revised.

This is an illustrative 20-page production budget, not fabricated story assignments or a promise that every week must contain 20 pages. Use only available, verified reporting and licensed media. A thin news week should produce a shorter strong issue rather than filler. Preserve each writer's voice and actual byline.

Visitor, subscriber and member flow

  1. A homepage, article or show visitor sees distinct actions: Get the free Friday newsletter and Read the complete weekly magazine. Avoid treating a free email signup as a paid membership.
  2. The free signup form explains the Friday email benefit and records consent through the existing approved process. Confirm successful subscription from the provider; a form click alone is not a signup. Preserve existing suppressions, unsubscribe behavior and send-approval rules.
  3. A public issue landing page shows the date, actual cover, contents, a useful sample and the paid benefits. It states which material is included and gives existing members a visible sign-in path.
  4. The plan comparison presents monthly and annual options together, the same magazine benefits, current prices, trial eligibility and billing/renewal terms. Display the full annual charge as well as any monthly equivalent. Do not preselect a paid option in a way that obscures the commitment.
  5. Checkout uses the existing native flow. Before payment confirmation, make trial length, first charge/date, later billing interval, renewal amount and applicable coupon limits clear. If a start-date selector appears, verify whether the live plan intentionally permits a custom start; do not label a legacy viewport or native step defective without a usability check.
  6. After a confirmed order, offer Read this week's issue, Download the issue and Manage membership. Existing members should reach content through their existing entitlement rather than being directed to buy again.
  7. The signed-in library shows current and past issues with consistent issue dates, readable covers, download availability and clear navigation. Verify access against the member's current entitlement. A public or permanent file URL must not accidentally bypass the intended download access.

Verify the journey at 390, 430 and 1366 px and check narrow-screen checkout usability directly. Root's pricing placeholder fix is live at embed revision 5; this specification does not imply other purchase friction has been repaired.

Renewal and account experience

Make Manage membership discoverable from the signed-in account, issue library, purchase confirmation and relevant renewal information. Resolve its actual native destination and verify it with an authorized signed-in test before publishing a URL or claiming a working self-service path.

The account view should expose the current plan, billing interval, next charge date/amount, trial end when applicable, payment method summary, available receipts and cancellation path. Display only data belonging to the authenticated member. Failed-payment recovery should use the provider's supported account flow; do not collect payment details in an email, issue comment or custom form.

ESPN's official payment-information guidance is a useful reference for making billing management discoverable and routing customers to the provider that owns their subscription. It does not establish that GatorBait supports the same account features or destinations. Verify the corresponding Wix capabilities and any external billing origin before promising them. No visual claims about The Athletic are used.

Before cancellation is confirmed, show its effective date and the corresponding access end date from current provider evidence. A scheduled cancellation at the next payment date is distinct from immediate cancellation and from an already ended entitlement. Do not silently change cancellation settings, renewal reminders, grace periods or access rules to implement this presentation.

Implementation order and acceptance

  1. Confirm the current product/entitlement map and billing capabilities without changing them. Save aggregate counts and an approved signed-in account-flow receipt privately.
  2. Produce one real pilot issue and its downloadable edition from available material. Check typography, rights/credits, sources, corrected links, file weight and archive navigation.
  3. Draft the public sample, free signup benefit and monthly/annual comparison using verified current commercial terms. Review existing-member paths so the launch cannot create a duplicate-purchase trap.
  4. Repair the analytics date feed and verify that event dates, counts and source joins correspond to a real test interval. Add only missing instrumentation justified by that check.
  5. Verify the end-to-end visitor and member flows without a real purchase or financial mutation. Obtain the required business approval for any later pricing, billing, membership or automation change. Publishing and email sends retain their existing approval boundaries.

Acceptance is a useful free Friday edition, a complete readable weekly member issue, a working authorized download/archive path, clear purchase/renewal terms and verified current-member access. Neither agent activity nor a populated dashboard proves this outcome.
