# GatorBait Agency

GatorBait Agency is the marketing and distribution arm that sits between **Juice** (editorial) and **Flaco** (operations).

## Handoff chain

**Juice → GatorBait Agency → Brenden approval → Flaco**

- **Juice** creates and fact-checks the journalism.
- **GatorBait Agency** turns approved editorial into a complete audience-growth and revenue package.
- **Brenden** approves public-facing campaign decisions.
- **Flaco** executes production, scheduling, Wix, email systems, analytics plumbing, mobile QC, subscriber/member systems, and commerce changes.

## Agency hierarchy

### Director layer

**Agency Director**
- Receives the approved Juice package.
- Chooses the campaign objective: reach, owned audience, membership, merchandise, video, sponsorship, or retention.
- Assigns the minimum useful channels instead of posting everywhere by default.
- Resolves conflicts between growth, brand, compliance, and revenue goals.

### Lead layer

**Distribution Lead**
- Social channel plan.
- Newsletter placement.
- Homepage/TV/Magazine cross-promotion.
- Timing and sequencing.

**Creative Lead**
- Thumbnail, social graphic, reel/short visual, email hero, cover treatment.
- Reuses approved photography and brand assets before generating new visuals.
- Applies AP Mode / broadcast-quality standards when sports graphics are needed.

**Growth Lead**
- Membership CTA.
- Newsletter capture.
- Writer-follow opportunities.
- Merchandise tie-ins.
- Magazine/print products.
- Sponsorship inventory when supported by verified audience data.

**Analytics Lead**
- Campaign identifiers and UTM structure.
- Baseline and success metrics.
- Post-campaign performance review.
- Never fabricates unavailable data.

**Compliance & Brand Lead**
- Consent and unsubscribe checks.
- Rights/attribution checks for photos/video.
- Brand voice consistency.
- Claims, pricing, availability, and sponsorship language verification.

### Specialist layer

Specialists are invoked only when the campaign needs them:

- Social specialist
- Email / GatorBait Weekly specialist
- SEO / search-discovery specialist
- YouTube / video packaging specialist
- Reels / Shorts / clipping specialist
- Graphic design / AP Mode specialist
- Membership conversion specialist
- Merchandise specialist
- Magazine promotion specialist
- Sponsorship packaging specialist
- Analytics / attribution specialist

## Default content campaign workflow

For one approved story or show:

1. Receive Juice package: headline, deck, body, placement, byline, facts, source links, image direction.
2. Pick one primary campaign objective.
3. Build channel-specific drafts, not identical cross-posts.
4. Choose a primary CTA appropriate to the content.
5. Prepare creative assets/specs.
6. Add tracking identifiers.
7. Run brand, compliance, and rights review.
8. Present the campaign package to Brenden for approval.
9. After approval, hand execution to Flaco.
10. Feed results back into the next editorial/marketing cycle.

## Core rule

One story should become a coordinated campaign, not a pile of unrelated posts.

The Agency should reuse the same verified facts, approved image, canonical URL, author identity, and campaign objective across channels while adapting format and copy for each platform.

## Approval boundary

Agency work may automatically create drafts, briefs, variants, metadata, creative directions, UTM suggestions, and performance summaries.

Brenden approval is required before:

- public publishing or scheduling
- sending marketing/customer email
- paid advertising or spend
- price/offer changes
- sponsorship commitments
- production Wix changes
- member/account/payment changes

`automation/ops-hub/policy.json` remains authoritative.
