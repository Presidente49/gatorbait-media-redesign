GatorBait Media is cutting the price of All Access Annual in half for Cyber Weekend. Enter code CYBER49 at checkout from Friday, Nov. 27, through Monday, Nov. 30, and your first year is $49 instead of $99.

All Access is everything GatorBait produces. That means every members-only story and column, Franz Beard's daily Thoughts of the Day, The Buddy Martin Show live Monday through Thursday at 9 p.m. ET, recruiting coverage in every sport, the subscriber chat room, GatorBait Magazine and the full podcast and video archive.

The terms

The $49 price covers your first year. After that, the membership renews at the regular $99 a year until you cancel. You can cancel any time before your renewal date from My Account.

All Access starts with a seven-day free trial, so the $49 charge comes when the trial ends.

The code works once per customer, on All Access Annual only.

Current members do not need to do anything. Nothing about your membership changes.

How to get it

Go to the membership page, choose All Access Annual and enter CYBER49 at checkout. The code stops working at 11:59 p.m. ET on Monday, Nov. 30.
