```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: finished premium sports-magazine editorial cover artwork for a Wix Blog article and social preview. Landscape 16:9, 1920x1080 composition, crisp enough for a magazine, legible on a phone.
Primary story: Franz Beard's Thoughts of the Day column during Florida–Georgia football rivalry week.

LARGE centered-left dominant main headline on two lines exactly "THOUGHTS OF" and "THE DAY". Clearly visible smaller editorial eyebrow exactly "FLORIDA–GEORGIA". Tasteful small bottom byline exactly "FRANZ BEARD". All type stays well within an 8 percent safe margin, readable when reduced; central crop should retain main headline.
No other words. No GatorBait masthead at top. No Buddy Martin Show wording. No broadcaster branding, no fabricated team logos, no helmets, no people, no jerseys, no sponsor, no year/rankings/records/scores/odds/date/kickoff/venue, no watermark. Don't mimic ESPN or other network trade dress. Give the football tangible realism within an unmistakably designed editorial illustration; polished, classy, award-caliber magazine composition.
```
