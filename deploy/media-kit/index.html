Four ways to sponsor

One sponsor per placement, so your name is never crowded by a competitor. Every package can start with a single game week.

Pick 'Em presenting sponsor

Weekly or season · new for South Carolina, Oct. 10

GatorBait Pick 'Em is a free weekly contest: fans call the exact score of the Florida game, climb a season leaderboard, and the weekly winner is named on The Buddy Martin Show.

  • "Presented by [your business]" with logo and link at the top of the contest page
  • Your prize line: "This week's prize: …" (you supply the prize, e.g. a gift card)
  • Named in the winner shout-out on The Buddy Martin Show
  • Logo on the weekly winner graphic posted to Facebook
  • Named in the Tuesday and Friday "make your pick" social posts

Why it works: fans come back every week to enter and check the board, so your name gets repeat views from the same local fans.

Buddy Martin Show segment

Per show or per week · Mon., Wed., Thu. 9 p.m. ET

A named segment of the show, live on YouTube and Facebook, with the replay staying up on both channels.

  • "[Segment] presented by [your business]" open and close, read by the host
  • Your logo on screen during the segment
  • Link in the YouTube and Facebook episode descriptions
  • Optional 30-second host read about your business

Why it works: a live, host-read mention from a trusted voice, with the replay keeping it in view long after the show airs.

Game-day scoreboard and game-flow chart

Per game or season · peak-traffic weekends

GatorBait's live scoreboard and its postgame game-flow chart (Florida's win probability from kickoff to the final) appear on the site and in the recap. The Missouri recap ran one as its lead image.

  • "Game flow presented by [your business]" on the chart image itself
  • "Presented by" line on the live scoreboard module on game day
  • Logo on the chart wherever it is shared on social

Why it works: game weekends are our busiest days; Sept. 26 and 27 drew 2,727 visits combined.

Newsletter sponsor

Per send or monthly · GatorBait Magazine email

One sponsor slot in the GatorBait Magazine email, which goes to fans who opted in.

  • "Presented by" line with logo and link near the top of the email
  • A short message in your words (about 40 words), edited to our style
  • We report what we can measure: delivered count and clicks on your link

Why it works: these readers asked to hear from us and confirmed by email. The paid members on the list are Gators fans who already pay for coverage.

How the suggested ranges were set

For GatorBait's eyes; remove this section before the kit goes to a sponsor if you prefer.

Website reach15,498 visits in 29 days is about 16,000 a month. At an assumed $10–$20 per 1,000 views for local display ads, one placement on every visit would be worth about $160–$320 a month. A "presented by" placement on one feature is worth less than that, which is why the range starts at $150 a week.
