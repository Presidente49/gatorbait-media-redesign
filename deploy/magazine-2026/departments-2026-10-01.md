## Pregame: Florida at Missouri

**Kickoff:** Saturday, Oct. 3, 3:30 p.m. ET, ABC. **Site:** Memorial Stadium (Faurot Field), Columbia, Mo., artificial surface. **Forecast (ESPN/AccuWeather, as of Thursday morning):** 71 degrees, no rain, gusts to 10 mph.

**Line (DraftKings, via ESPN):** Florida -5.5; over/under 57.5; money line Florida -218, Missouri +180. Florida was the favorite at the open. ESPN's Matchup Predictor gives Florida a 70.2% chance.

**Series:** The teams have not met since Nov. 18, 2023, when No. 9 Missouri beat Florida 33-31 in Columbia. Before that: Florida 24-17 in Gainesville on Oct. 8, 2022, and Missouri 24-23 in Columbia on Nov. 20, 2021. The all-time series record was not available from ESPN.

Last week in Starkville, Missouri lost 31-24 without committing a turnover: 414 total yards (295 passing, 119 rushing), 7-of-16 on third down and 0-for-2 on fourth, while Mississippi State piled up 499 yards, 360 through the air. Austin Simmons went 23-of-39 for 295 yards and three touchdowns, Jamal Roberts ran 26 times for 95 yards and Cayden Lee caught 11 passes for 156 yards and two scores. Missouri hosts Texas A&M on Oct. 10.

**Missouri numbers:** 35.8 points (8th SEC) and 418.8 yards (10th) per game; 155.8 rushing (10th) at 4.2 per carry; 263.0 passing (7th) at 8.6 per attempt; 11 passing TD, no interceptions; two giveaways (fewest in the SEC). Defense: 20.8 points (7th SEC, T-51st nationally) and 318.5 yards (9th) allowed; 100.3 rushing allowed at 2.8 per carry (3rd SEC, 19th nationally) with two rushing TDs allowed; 218.3 passing allowed at 7.0 per attempt and 56.0% completions (23rd nationally); 12 sacks; 20 TFL; opponents 19-of-59 (32.2%) on third down; only two takeaways (fewest in the SEC). Leaders: QB Austin Simmons 70-of-107, 947 yards, 11 TD; RB Jamal Roberts 89-395, 3 TD; WR Cayden Lee 27-412, 4 TD; EDGE Daeden Hopkins 6 sacks; LB Nicholas Rodriguez 39 tackles.

**Three statistical keys**

1. **Baugh against a run defense that has not bent.** Florida runs for 260.0 yards a game (2nd in the SEC, 11th nationally) at 5.9 a carry, and its 18 rushing touchdowns are tied for the most in the country; Jadan Baugh has 600 yards and 11 scores and has topped 100 in every game. Missouri gives up 100.3 rushing yards a game and 2.8 a carry (3rd in the SEC, 19th nationally) and has allowed two rushing touchdowns in four games.

2. **Chunk plays against a secondary that just got torched.** Florida averages 10.5 yards per attempt (1st in the SEC, T-6th nationally), completes 73.1% (7th nationally) and has allowed two sacks in 104 dropbacks. Missouri holds opponents to 7.0 per attempt and 56.0% completions, but Mississippi State threw for 360 on it last week, and Daeden Hopkins' six sacks are the pass rush Florida must account for.

3. **Florida's pass defense and third downs against Simmons.** The Gators allow 248.3 passing yards a game (15th of 16 SEC teams, 113th nationally) and let opponents convert 40.6% on third down (13th in the SEC). Simmons has 947 yards and 11 touchdowns without an interception, and Cayden Lee has 412 yards. The turnover ledger cuts both ways: Florida is +3 with seven takeaways; Missouri has given it away twice (fewest in the SEC) but has taken it away only twice (also fewest). Jayden Woods (19 tackles, 6 TFL, 4 sacks) is the Gator most likely to change that math.
