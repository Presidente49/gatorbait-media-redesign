Brenden's service direction · Oct. 19, 2026 · Proposed offering

One connected team.
Your company’s voice.

A managed publishing and customer-engagement service: turn approved source material into checked articles, useful email and native social, then measure what brings people back.

GatorBait is the first working proof. Client rollout follows a verified complete delivery cycle and account readiness check. This page defines the service; it does not form a legal company or claim a deployed platform for every business.

The first offer

Set up and operate one repeatable content workflow for a business that already has stories, customers and working publishing accounts. One accountable controller, a shared queue, creative support and independent checks.

Deliverables: a branded email template, source-to-publication workflow, social artwork and copy, delivery receipts, correction handling and a concise results dashboard.

Make it reusable

  • Separate each client’s source inbox, brand kit, accounts, audience, approvals and private records.
  • Connect the client’s existing CMS, email and social tools through supported APIs.
  • Keep a common queue and evidence format; adapt the publishing details to each platform.
  • Client-owned accounts and documented exports make handoff straightforward.

Keep it profitable

  • Use deterministic checks and existing tools first; call models for judgment and creative work.
  • One controller and a small number of bounded helpers. No continuous token-consuming loops.
  • Log observed model/tool costs, human time and rework per accepted item.
  • Propose setup plus monthly service pricing after measuring GatorBait’s workload and costs. Brenden sets the price.

The proof before the pitch

  1. Restore authorized access to the newsroom inbox and existing Meta session.
  2. Take one real writer filing through byline/fact checks, publication, live QC, native email and native social.
  3. Record actual delivery, qualified visits, signups and attributed paid conversions where tracking supports them.
  4. Measure operating cost and correction rate. Turn that evidence into a small client pilot with a defined scope.

No invented engagement, universal compatibility claim, autonomous account reconnection or paid service purchase. Permissions and measurable client outcomes travel with the workflow.
