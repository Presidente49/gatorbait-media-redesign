# The Road Ahead (preview, not live)

An additive band for the homepage: one snap-scroll ribbon of Florida's 2026 schedule with a glowing route that draws to the next game, win-margin bars on finals, a live kickoff countdown, and the ranked opponents (Texas, Georgia) lit as the tough stretch. Barlow only, night palette, no images, reduced-motion safe. 6,984 characters (fits a Wix embed with room to spare).

- `module.html`: the band, self-contained.
- `preview.html`: the band on a dark page for review.
- Data is embedded from `sports-live/scoreboard.json` (ESPN). A live version should read that feed the way Front Page 2026 does.

Nothing here is live. The homepage look is locked; placing this band needs Brenden's explicit yes.
