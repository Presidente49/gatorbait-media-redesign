Kickoff & Injury Watch

3:30 p.m. ET, ABC — No. 21 Florida (3-0, 1-0 SEC) hosts No. 4 Ole Miss (3-0, 1-0 SEC) in the SEC home opener.

Florida: WR Eric Singleton Jr. (ankle) is questionable, a game-time decision for the third straight week. Coach Sumrall says there's a "real good shot" he plays. If not, Vernell Brown III starts.

Ole Miss: RB Kewan Lacy (shoulder) is a game-time decision after last week's LSU game; reports on his exact status vary, but coach Golding says the MRI came back better than feared. JT Lindsay would step in if Lacy sits.
