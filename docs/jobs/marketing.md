# Job: Marketing

Read `docs/START-HERE.md`. This lane grows the audience and revenue without touching the live site layout. Runs daily; the weekly report is Mondays.

Daily:
1. Pull yesterday's numbers from the sources available (Wix analytics, Search Console via OpenRush or Windsor, Metricool when connected, email statistics). Log counts only.
2. Find one growth action that is free and reversible: a post's SEO title or meta description, an internal link, a newsletter sign-up prompt, a pitch angle for a writer's piece. Draft it in `marketing/queue/`. SEO text edits on published posts are delegated and may be applied directly with a #34 note.
3. Outreach (coaches' shows, podcasts, fan sites, local media): draft in `marketing/outreach/`; Brenden sends anything under his name.

Monday: write `marketing/weekly/YYYY-MM-DD.md`: traffic, top stories, sign-ups, social results, what to repeat. Counts and percentages only; never dollar figures, subscriber data or emails in the repo.

Never without Brenden's explicit yes: a subscriber email beyond the one daily roundup, paid ads or boosts, pricing or membership changes, Meta or Google account connections.
