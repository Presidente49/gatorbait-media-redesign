# GatorBait social rules

Standing direction from Brenden Martin (president), Oct. 4, 2026:

> "It's critical that no matter when we do social media that we're always sharing one story at a time, giving attribution and creating within the social media app so the algorithm favors it. If we can have our own picture, even better ... that way the writers feel like they have their own [piece]."

These rules apply to every post on every GatorBait account, from every person, routine and agent.

## The rules
1. **One story per post.** Each post promotes one article, one clip or one show. No roundup posts that link five stories, and no "here's everything from today" posts.
2. **Credit the writer every time.** Put the writer's name in the post copy ("By Franz Beard" or "Franz Beard breaks down..."), and tag their handle on that platform when they have one. Buddy Martin's pieces credit Buddy. Staff pieces credit GatorBait.
3. **Create natively in the app.**
   - Upload the photo, card or video directly to the platform. Write the copy in the post itself.
   - Don't share a bare link or use a link-preview post as the main post.
   - Put the story link where that platform expects it: the first comment, link sticker or bio link. Use the post itself only where a link doesn't hurt reach.
   - Scheduling tools (Metricool and the like) are fine when they publish native media posts, not link shares.
4. **Use our own picture when we have one.** Rank images in this order:
   1. a GatorBait photographer's shot, credited "Photo: <name>, GatorBait Media";
   2. a GatorBait-made graphic (game-flow chart, stat card, quote card);
   3. a properly licensed or credited image.
   Never post an uncredited image grabbed from another outlet.
5. **Facts and quotes follow site rules.** Stats come from ESPN or FloridaGators.com, and quotes come from the full source. The post can't claim more than the story does.
6. **Give each writer room.** Space posts so each writer's story has its own window. Don't bury one writer's piece minutes after another's.

## Posting checklist
- [ ] One story
- [ ] Writer named (and tagged if they have a handle)
- [ ] Native media upload, with copy written for this platform
- [ ] Link in the platform's preferred spot, not as a link-preview post
- [ ] Our own photo or graphic, credited
- [ ] Stats and quotes match the published story

## Limits
- No paid boosts or ad spend without Brenden's explicit approval.
- No connecting or reconnecting Meta, Google or TikTok accounts without Brenden.
- Bylines stay honest. Never attribute a story to a writer who didn't write it.
