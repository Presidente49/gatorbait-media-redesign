# Photo attribution (Brenden, Oct. 18, 2026)

UAA's biggest concern is attribution, so we go further than required. This applies to every UAA / UF photo (Hannah White and any other UF photographer) on the site, in email, in the magazine and on social.

1. **Credit text:** "Photo courtesy UAA and Hannah White." Use the photographer named in the file (for example `261017_Texas_FB_11098_HannahWhite.jpg`). Never crop, cover or strip a credit.
2. **Link both credits.** UAA links to https://floridagators.com. The photographer links to their own page when one exists (Hannah White: https://www.instagram.com/hannahmorganphoto/). Add a courtesy link to UF's official galleries (https://floridagators.com/galleries, or the game's gallery once UF posts it).
3. **Site stories:** credit in the cover/image alt text, plus a visible closing line: "Photo courtesy UAA (University Athletic Association, FloridaGators.com) and Hannah White. See more of her work on Instagram, and the official Florida Gators photo galleries at FloridaGators.com." with the three links.
4. **Email and magazine:** credit in the alt text and a linked "Photo credits" block (email) or a caption on each photo plus the footer (magazine).
5. **Social shares:** the credit is on the image itself (small line, bottom corner) and in the caption text: "Photo courtesy UAA and Hannah White (floridagators.com)", plus a link to floridagators.com/galleries where the platform allows links. Tag the account only if the handle is verified.
6. We do not host a GatorBait gallery of UAA photos. We link to theirs.
7. Get the photo from UF's own pages (recap page or gallery), import it into Wix Media Manager, set alt text with the credit, and record the source in #34.
