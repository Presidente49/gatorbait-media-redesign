Broadcast

SEC Network graphics energy · navy field, orange hits

The homepage behaves like a pregame show: a score bug and a ticker are the first things you see, and every story is a numbered rundown item rather than a card. Angled cuts, tabular numerals and a hard white matchup band give it the density of a broadcast graphics package without a single rounded corner.

What we'd build firstThe ticker + score bug + matchup band as one live component fed by the existing ESPN read, then the numbered rundown under it. Motion: ticker scroll, flip-clock countdown, score-bug wipe on load.

Magazine

Editorial front page · paper, rules, big photography

A real front page: a navy masthead over warm paper, a 12-column grid where the lead photograph and a 88px Barlow Condensed headline share the fold, and columnists get a rail instead of a card. Rules, drop caps and a full-width pull quote do the work that boxes and shadows do everywhere else; the Thursday cover sits in the rail as the Magazine's front door, not the homepage's.

What we'd build firstThe masthead + 12-column lead/secondary/rail grid with the columnists rail and the cover panel, fed by the existing newest-first feed with Buddy pinned to the lead. Motion: masthead settles on load, lead photo slow Ken Burns, rail items reveal on scroll.

Swamp Night

Cinematic game night · ember glow, glass, neon orange

The Swamp after dark: one full-bleed Chris Spears frame under a deep navy-to-black gradient, an ember glow rising from the bottom corner, and a glass kickoff card counting down to Columbia. Type is big and quiet, orange is reserved for the glow and the numbers, and the stories sit in a live desk instead of a grid of thumbnails.

What we'd build firstThe hero + kickoff card + score strip as one game-week module that flips to a live state on Saturday, then the desk list. Motion: ember drift in the background, pulsing live dot, countdown ticking, gallery auto-advancing with a glow sweep.
