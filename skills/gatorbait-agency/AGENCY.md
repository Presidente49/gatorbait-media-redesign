# GatorBait Agency

GatorBait Agency is the marketing and distribution arm that sits between **Juice** (editorial) and **Flaco** (operations).

## Handoff chain

**Juice → Graphic Design Artist / Creative Director → GatorBait Agency → Flaco**

- **Juice** creates and fact-checks the journalism.
- **Graphic Design Artist / Creative Director** turns the verified story into a resolved master visual when creative is needed, using `skills/gatorbait-graphic-design/SKILL.md`.
- **GatorBait Agency** turns the verified editorial + master creative into a complete audience-growth and revenue package.
- **Flaco** executes production, scheduling, Wix, email systems, analytics plumbing, mobile QC, subscriber/member systems, and commerce changes.

Routine organic work follows the current autopilot policy in `automation/ops-hub/policy.json`; only matters outside those boundaries escalate to Brenden.

## Agency hierarchy

### Director layer

**Agency Director**
- Receives the verified Juice package and, when required, the approved master creative.
- Chooses the campaign objective: reach, owned audience, membership, merchandise, video, sponsorship, or retention.
- Assigns the minimum useful channels instead of posting everywhere by default.
- Resolves conflicts between growth, brand, compliance, and revenue goals.

### Lead layer

**Distribution Lead**
- Social channel plan.
- Newsletter placement.
- Homepage/TV/Magazine cross-promotion.
- Timing and sequencing.

**Creative Lead / Creative Director**
- Invokes `skills/gatorbait-graphic-design/SKILL.md` for magazine covers, thumbnails, social graphics, reel/short visuals, email heroes, print/PDF layouts, and AP Mode/broadcast graphics.
- Reuses approved photography and brand assets before generating new visuals.
- Produces one strong master creative direction before channel derivatives.
- Keeps interactive web controls in the web layer rather than baking them into artwork.
- Applies AP Mode / broadcast-quality standards when sports graphics are needed.

**Growth Lead**
- Membership CTA.
- Newsletter capture.
- Writer-follow opportunities.
- Merchandise tie-ins.
- Magazine/print products.
- Sponsorship inventory when supported by verified audience data.

**Analytics Lead**
- Campaign identifiers and UTM structure.
- Baseline and success metrics.
- Post-campaign performance review.
- Never fabricates unavailable data.

**Compliance & Brand Lead**
- Consent and unsubscribe checks.
- Rights/attribution checks for photos/video.
- Brand voice consistency.
- Claims, pricing, availability, and sponsorship language verification.

### Specialist layer

Specialists are invoked only when the campaign needs them:

- Social specialist
- Email / GatorBait Weekly specialist

- Reels / Shorts / clipping specialist
- Graphic Design Artist / AP Mode specialist

## Default content campaign workflow

For one verified story or show:

1. Receive Juice package: headline, deck, body, placement, byline, facts, source links, image direction.
2. If the campaign needs a key visual, Graphic Design Artist creates the master creative and completes visual QA.
3. Pick one primary campaign objective.
4. Build channel-specific drafts, not identical cross-posts.
5. Choose a primary CTA appropriate to the content.
6. Adapt the master creative for the required channels.
7. Add tracking identifiers.
8. Run brand, compliance, rights, and destination checks.
9. Hand execution to Flaco inside the current autopilot/approval boundaries.
10. Feed results back into editorial, creative, and marketing learning loops.

## Core rule

One story should become a coordinated campaign, not a pile of unrelated posts.

The Agency should reuse the same verified facts, approved image/master creative, canonical URL, author identity, and campaign objective across channels while adapting format and copy for each platform.

## Approval boundary

`automation/ops-hub/policy.json` is authoritative.

Routine organic editorial/marketing/creative may proceed automatically after its required gates pass. Escalate paid spend, pricing/benefit changes, refunds, credentials/DNS/payment changes, contracts/sponsorship commitments, sensitive legal claims, and other items explicitly listed as approval-required in policy.
