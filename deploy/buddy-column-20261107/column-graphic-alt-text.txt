LONG DESCRIPTION (for the magazine PDF tag, figure text or an image description field)
Typographic graphic for Buddy Martin's GatorBait Media column. The top of a navy watch dial fills the frame, with cream tick marks and one orange tick at 12 o'clock. Orange type curves along the dial: "Recruiting starts now." Large cream headline: "The Monster That's Never Fed." with "Fed." in orange. Below it, in italic: "A Football Coaches' Lament," then "By Buddy Martin." Two cream watch hands sweep in from the bottom corners. No photograph and no people appear in the image.

NOTES
- Every word on the art comes from the column: the title, the line "the monster is tapping its watch: recruiting starts now," and the byline. No stat, score, date or name other than Buddy Martin appears on the art.
- Straight apostrophes follow the house preference; the live title uses curly ones.
