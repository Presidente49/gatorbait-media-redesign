Something’s Stirring in The Swamp. And It’s Waking Up The Echoes.


On Friday, Paul Finebaum brought his TV show to campus and spent an hour ringing some of the sweetest chimes since Century Tower was at its best.



By Buddy Martin

GatorBaitMedia.com


Maya Angelou never sat in the press box at Florida Field, which is probably lucky for her. The coffee was terrible and the view has gotten worse for about 20 years.


Still, she probably would have understood this place better than a lot of the people who got paid to fix it.


This week in Gainesville, it was about the feeling.


An Hour Worth Remembering


Afterward, Paul told me he thought it was “a really good hour” of television. Coming from a man who has spent decades listening to SEC callers explain how their team got robbed, that’s close to a papal blessing.


Look at who else was in that lineup.


Some men get old. Spurrier just gets more Spurrier.


Why It Feels Different


Feelings like this get earned.


Florida beat Auburn 44-39 in a game that could have gone badly and didn’t.


About Saturday


I don’t know what happens Saturday night when No. 4 Ole Miss comes to town to play No. 21 Florida. Nobody does. Ole Miss is ranked fourth for a reason, and good vibes have never blocked a blitz.


They remembered how it feels to be Florida.
